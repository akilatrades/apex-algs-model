    Teams that missed the Championship

    Every rated team that played Split 2 Pro League, the Split 2 Playoffs or an LCQ final and is not in the field, strongest first. The rating is the points per game we would expect from the team in a Championship lobby (the field averages about ). "Would rank" places that rating among the 40 qualified teams.

    How the rating works: it is the points per game we expect from a team in a Championship lobby, worked out from each game's full finishing order across Year 5 Champs finals, Year 6 Playoffs, Split 2 Pro League and LCQ finals. Teams with thin records are pulled toward the average for their region, and teams with no LAN record are marked down. A gap of half a point per game is small. Part of each rating follows the players, so roster moves count, and teams that usually share a landing spot with another team in the lobby get a penalty on that map. Names are as listed on apexlegendsstatus.com. Scoring: 12/9/7/5/4/3/3/2/2/2/1×5 placement points, +1 per kill.

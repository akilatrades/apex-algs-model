How much should you trust this?

Use it as a guide, not a crystal ball. It beats guessing, but pro Apex is close and a lot comes down to the day.

How we tested it

We rated teams using only results from before the Split 2 Playoffs, then compared that with what actually happened there (63 games).

Bars show the points a game each group actually scored. The teams we rated highest did score the most. One caution: we used this same event to fine-tune things, so it may do a little worse on new games. In Pro League, the team we rated higher finished ahead of the time.

What's new in this update

    What it doesn't know

    • Player swaps on teams that kept the same name. Plenty of rosters changed between splits.
    • The real Champs groups and format, which haven't been announced.
    • How teams have been practising lately, beyond 12 scrim games.
    How it works, and where the numbers come from

    The ranking. Every game's full finishing order counts, not just who won. Finishing 3rd ahead of 17 other teams tells us something. Teams with only a few games are pulled toward the average for their region, so one lucky weekend can't send a team to the top. Teams who've never played a LAN get a small deduction, sized by how much first-time LAN teams dropped off last time.

    The odds. We play out the whole Championship 8,000 times: groups, the elimination rounds, then the match-point final, using each team's ranking plus a realistic amount of luck. A team's chance is simply how often it won. Groups are drawn at random, since the real ones aren't out yet.

    Checks. Every game's results and every team's totals match the stats site, including the site's own season totals for all team-and-event records. We also checked the maths behind the odds with a set of automatic tests.
